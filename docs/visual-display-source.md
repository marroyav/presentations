# Source notes: The Visual Display of Quantitative Information

The plotting and diagram guidance uses Edward R. Tufte’s *The Visual Display
of Quantitative Information*, second edition, Graphics Press. The downloaded
scan includes a second-edition preface dated January 2001; its filename says
2000. The filename alone should not establish the publication year.

Local source reviewed on 5 November 2026:

`~/Downloads/Edward Rolf Tufte - The Visual Display of Quantitative Information, 2nd Edition-Graphics Press (2000).pdf`

The PDF contains 191 scanned pages and no useful extracted text. The pages below
were inspected as images. PDF page numbers count from one; printed numbers
refer to the book. The offset changes, so do not assume one offset throughout.
The book and its page images are not copied into this repository.

| Passage reviewed | Printed page | PDF page | Application here |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Graphical Excellence, opening | 13 | 10 | Show evidence, enable comparison, integrate explanation |
| Graphical Integrity, six principles | 77 | 74 | Preserve proportion, label clearly, retain context |
| Data-Ink, contrasting scatter plots | 95 | 90 | Keep data visible; remove competing grid and redundant marks |
| Chartjunk, chapter opening | Chapter 5 opening | 102 | Remove decoration that adds no information |
| Small-multiple repair records | 174 | 167 | Use repeated forms for comparisons |
| Data/text integration | 181 | 174 | Place words, tables, and graphics together |
| Aspect ratio and labeling | 187 | 180 | Choose geometry that supports reading and meaning |

The guides paraphrase these ideas. White slides, projector-size typography,
vector export, uncertainty reporting, engineering provenance, and the extension
to system diagrams are local implementation choices. The book does not establish
a universal white-background rule or a slide template. Its dense printed examples
should not be copied onto slides at unreadable sizes.

Use the [plotting guide](plotting-guide.md) for quantitative evidence and the
[diagram guide](diagram-guide.md) for relationships. Read the
[schematic framework](schematic-framework.md) for electrical authority and checks.
