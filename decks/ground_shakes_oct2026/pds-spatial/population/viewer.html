What counts as an event in these plots?

At least 12 connected channels across 4 physical modules must have usable samples. The largest response must be at least 20 ADC counts from baseline. Each event contributes once; equal maxima in different modules are excluded.

First and second early pulses are the first two existing sharp-pulse candidates earlier than −100 µs. The original search extends to −4 ms where samples exist. Fixed snapshots span 0.512 µs. Pulse origins are not classified as light.

Missing or rail-flagged samples are excluded, not replaced with zero. Module 8 contains reported disconnected channels 11, 13, 14 and 16. Gains and electronics delays are not calibrated.
