VD Detector Protection System — fast speaking script
I&I meeting, November 2026

15 main slides; 266 spoken words. Allow about four minutes.
Backups 16–53 are for questions.

1. VD Detector Protection System

I’ll cover our starting point for the VD DPS, and the work needed for a full conceptual design.

2. The hardware connection list is still growing

We know rack protection and BDE. We still need the complete hardware connection list.

3. DPS protects equipment; SC operates and records

DPS protects equipment. Slow controls operates and records; local interlocks can act independently.

4. Facility interfaces may connect to SC or the DPS PLC

Facility interfaces may connect to SC/Ignition, the DPS PLC, or both. Cryogenics exchanges information in both directions.

5. Build the DPS brain on the BDE PLC architecture

We propose the BDE PLC approach: industrial hardware, modular expansion and vendor support, with a separate EtherCAT field network.

6. Raritan PDU controls on dune-fd-test01

Our Raritan demo will show individual outlet control, local rules or Lua scripts, and a fault/permit bit sent through a compatible contact actuator.

7. FFDG BMS and SDSTA SCADA integration is progressing

We’re progressing with both interfaces. FFDG BMS can inform operator decisions; hard interlocks are not assumed. The SDSTA work includes Oro Hondo ventilation status.

8. Cryogenics presents one system to DUNE

Cryogenics keeps its own controls. We need agreed readiness states and responses to missing signals.

9. Trace each interlock from its source to the response

Trace each interlock from its source through the connection, decision, action and feedback.

10. Each group must identify its hardware connections

We need each consortium and LBNF to identify their connections, requirements, gaps and contact.

11. A trip removes the permit; recovery needs a new request

A trip removes the permit. Reset and re-enable are separate actions.

12. BDE is nearly complete; PDS has two decisions

BDE is nearly complete. PDS must assess purity-monitor inhibition versus data tagging, and define its PoF inhibition path.

13. Agree the CalCI, HV and TDE protection interfaces

CalCI and PDS must agree the laser handshake. HVS needs mapped cryogenic inputs and shutdown dependencies; TDE needs defined PDU trips and feedback.

14. Use Batavia and SURF benches to test the protection paths

Complete the Batavia demonstrator and expand the SURF setup. Test faults, inhibits, feedback, response time, communications loss and recovery on both benches.

15. Gather inputs by January; complete the design by April

Gather consortium and LBNF information by late January 2027. Complete the comprehensive DPS design by mid-April 2027, supported by the two benches.

16. Backups — after the main talk

The detector walkthrough, full matrix and planning details are available for questions.

22. Detector

Now, the detector systems.

23. Ten BDE groups use the existing bottom-drift design

BDE provides the starting design: ten groups, eighty warm crates and dedicated supply inhibits.

24. Extend the BDE approach into a full DPS concept

We propose to extend that PLC and remote-I/O approach once the additional interfaces are mapped.

25. A cooling fault removes power from the affected crate

A fan fault inhibits power to the affected crate. Associated bias shutdown still needs agreement.

26. SC and DAQ use distinct DAPHNE service paths

SC and DAQ’s CCM use the OPC UA path; Hermes has a separate configuration interface.

27. PDS must define the PoF inhibition path

PDS must define PoF inhibition. PDU switching and a box-enable input are candidates.

28. LV supplies power DAPHNE and PDS calibration boxes

For DAPHNE and calibration boxes, the working LV scope is thirty-four feeds; channel and inhibit assignments remain open.

29. Drift HV and bias supplies have separate inhibit paths

The HV design has separate wired inhibits for drift HV and CRP/field-cage bias.

30. Trace each protection input to a temperature sensor

Each protection measurement needs a sensor location, limit, response time and affected load.

31. Proposed laser rules block conflicting PDS requests

The proposed laser rule blocks conflicting PDS requests. Local laser-safety interlocks remain independent.

32. The purity-monitor inhibit rule still needs agreement

We still need to agree whether purity-monitor activity needs tagging or PDS bias inhibition.

33. Measure the full path from fault to confirmed shutdown

We plan to test fault detection through confirmed shutdown, including communications loss, recovery and logging.

49. Use rack sensors and PDUs for shared protection

Rack sensors and PDUs provide shared protection. For TDE, our current DPS scope is PDU control.
