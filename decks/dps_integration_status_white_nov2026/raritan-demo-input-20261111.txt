Use the Raritan PDU connected in the dune-fd-test01 setup to explain a proposed
demonstration for the collaboration: individual socket control, discrete contact
signals to PLC logic or other equipment, and programmable local PDU logic.

Keep the original rack/TDE protection slide as a backup so its prior hardware

Technical distinction for the new sketch:
- Contact-closure inputs acquire smoke/switch status.
- A compatible output-capable dry-contact actuator can switch a discrete circuit
  into a matched PLC or other device input. It conveys a fault/permit bit, not a
  stream of sensor measurements. Do not treat the smoke-input relay as that output.
- Individual AC outlet switching is distinct from a low-voltage contact signal.
- Raritan documents event rules and LuaPLC for local automation. The fitted
  accessory, firmware, enabled features and PLC input must be checked on the setup.

This is a demo plan. No PDU outlet was switched, no PLC variable written, and no
new installed capability or completed protection test is claimed.
