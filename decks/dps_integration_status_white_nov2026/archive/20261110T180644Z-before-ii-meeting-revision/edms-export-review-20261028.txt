How the deck uses this
- BDE description and trip example retain the physical PLC / EtherCAT / inhibit paths.
- Device hierarchy now includes fan/heater outputs, environmental inputs,
  power terminals and fieldbus extension/fibre components.
- Procurement preparation includes software licences.
- The VD fan-to-bias dependency and shutdown order remain to be agreed.

Limits and differences that remain visible in the source notes
- These 2020 documents describe Detector 1 / HD: 150 warm crates, 25 LV groups,
  5 bias groups and 658 bias channels are not substituted for the VD counts.
- The older DDSS parts sheet lists CX5120 and EL2088 bias outputs. The CE sheet
  used previously lists CX5140 and EL2124, and contains April 2023 quotes.
  Those variants are not treated as one current approved bill of materials.
- Historical material shows four fans per flange; the later six-fan WIEC case
  is a conditional allocation check, not a claim of 48 new inputs per VD group.
- Historical missing-feedback statements do not qualify today's proposed SNMP
  readback path. End-to-end timing and feedback still need bench tests.
- Old heater thresholds are proposals, not VD operating settings.
- Drawings and presentations are design references, not evidence of installation.
