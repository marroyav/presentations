Current architecture

The presentation restores the definitions and hierarchy from the original
talk, with Manuel's explicit corrections. DPS receives inputs from FFDG BMS,
SDSTA BMS, cryogenics and detector. SC and the subsystems sit within detector.
SC operates, monitors and owns logging. SC communicates with the DPS PLC
over OPC UA. The PLC arbitrates protection and sends permits/inhibits to the
subsystem interfaces; conditions and faults return to the PLC. The PDU
control chain is Ignition → DPS PLC → PDU, with independent local interlocks.
There is no change of ownership between Ignition and the PLC: they are stages
of that chain. PDS LV count is 34.

These inputs are recorded in architecture-inputs-20261027.txt. They govern the
presentation when an older working note or previous assistant draft differs.
The original definitions are preserved in the archived 23 October main.tex.
The older SC–DPS interface, EDMS 2907050 v1 pp4–5, supplies the historical
system definitions. The supplied email and current instructions give the
logging and arbitration roles used here.

Existing BDE engineering

The May VD rack workbook allocates 20 BDE LV racks, 10 BDE auxiliary racks
and 10 bias-HV racks. Each auxiliary and bias rack has 12U for DPS. The bias
sheet identifies its design as a first pass based on the earlier detector.
These are rack-space allocations, not claims of completed installed panels.

The June SC layout and Ignition screenshot show ten BDE groups. Each group
contains two PL506 supplies and eight WIECs. The BDE ICD supplies six WIBs per
WIEC: 80 WIECs/PTCs and 480 WIBs overall. I/O, PTC, WIEC and WIB definitions
are now in the main talk before the proposed extension. A rack, I/O group,
coupler and physical panel remain separate quantities.

EDMS 2401090 v4 contains the TPC panel drawing, CE Interlock Parts List and
DDSS Controls Power Analysis. The drawing names CX5120; the April 2023 parts
list names CX5140. Other listed components include EK1100, EK1521 and EL2124,
with RTD and fan/heater I/O, supplies and an OPC UA licence. The older 25 LV /
five bias rack populations and historical costs are not VD purchase totals.
The June 2026 Ignition screen is evidence of the existing front end, not a
claim that every VD group has been commissioned.

Parts and power applied to VD

The VD bias-panel draft has two 337 mm DIN rails per rack. Per rail it lists
1 EK1100, 1 EL9410, 1 EL9011, 18 EL2124 and one 24 V supply. Ten racks imply
20 rails, 360 EL2124 modules and 1,440 output channels in that first-pass
layout. The working VD inventory has 576 bias channels. The output-to-inhibit
mapping sets the purchase count; spare installed capacity is not established.

BDE LV RACK!D27 gives 2,254 W expected AC input per PL506, with four WIECs
and the workbook efficiency assumption. Twenty supplies imply 45.08 kW.
BDE Aux Rack!D14 gives 831 W; K13:K14 identify its six-WIEC origin and the
approximately 33% increase for eight. Linear planning scaling gives 1,108 W
per auxiliary rack, or 11.08 kW for ten. This is not a detailed new field-load
calculation. Bias HV Rack!K22 gives 24 V x 40 A = 960 W DC capacity, a
different quantity from expected AC demand. No combined DPS input total is
claimed.

PDS count and monitoring population

The current presentation uses PDS LV = 34, supplied directly by Manuel. The
older PDS working inventory is used for the 80 PoF boxes and eight temperature
plus eight voltage readings per box. It is not used to reconstruct the current
LV count or a new MPOD module allocation. The 1,280 PoF monitoring values size
local readout; permits, fault status and shutdown groups determine DPS I/O.

Arbitration and local PDU protection

Ignition controls the DPS PLC; the PLC arbitrates and controls the PDU. Local
interlocks remain capable of tripping. Equipment state and trip feedback return
to the PLC, while SC owns the logs. Reset and re-enable are distinct actions.
The diagram shows this chain explicitly and does not imply direct Ignition
bypass of the PLC or an additional DPS long-term logging system.

The PX3 guide supplies concrete reference local functions: smoke, water and
temperature rules, a retained network-switch outlet during fallback shutdown,
and deliberate restoration. These capabilities inform the prototype. They do
not establish the completed VD configuration or override the stated chain.
The earlier interpretation of this as a choice between Ignition and PLC
ownership has been removed from the active deck and notes.

Interfaces and testing

The existing matrix contains hardware-relevant requirements and conflicts.
The purity-monitor/PDS light and bias example changes which permits and
feedback are needed, so it affects hardware before the full response matrix
is complete. Manuel clarifies that current TDE control is through its PDUs
only. Crate status and an external inhibit remain possible future extensions,
retained in the proposal without claiming that they are available. Cryogenic instrumentation can supply
level information; its origin is not assigned to cryogenic controls by the
generic low-level/HV example.

The June BDE/VST work and July G271 record supply existing test context. G271
then listed PDU, PL506, mini-MPOD and switch as installed, with WIEC/PLC still
planned. Test arbitration, local trips, the complete detection-to-shutdown
time, SC/fieldbus loss, feedback, logging, reset and re-enable. The simulator
prepares cases but does not qualify PLC, fieldbus or actuator timing.

Location and site work

The central VD PLC is proposed in the CUC beside the other PLC racks. The
source detector-control diagrams still label its rack TBD. The proposal diagram now separates the CUC controller from the detector-rack
hardware: existing BDE/bias I/O on EtherCAT, additional VD interfaces through
SC over OPC UA, and direct PDU control/status from the DPS PLC. SC uses OPC UA
to the PLC. The design must supply the actual
fieldbus route, control power and network dependencies.
The facility maps contain twenty control-panel symbols, including eight CUC
references and two sump interfaces at 4910. Field observations are pending.
The mapped symbols are not a claim of twenty inspected installed cabinets.
