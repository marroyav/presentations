Definitions and responsibilities
DPS                     Detector Protection System: arbitrates protection, applies permits and inhibits, and
                        verifies the equipment response.
SC                      Slow controls, using Ignition: operates and monitors equipment, coordinates re-
                        quests, and owns the logs.
DAQ                     Configures readout, takes data, and uses detector availability and activity from SC.
BMS / cryo              Building Management Systems and cryogenic controls own their services and pro-
                        vide agreed states to SC.
Subsystems              Define operating limits, required protection and allowed response times.
Local                   Equipment-level protection: can trip without SC or the central DPS.
