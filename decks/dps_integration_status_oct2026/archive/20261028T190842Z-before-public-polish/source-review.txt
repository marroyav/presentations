BMS service boundary
The FFDG–SDSTA MOU, DocDB 9333 v5, identifies SDSTA as SURF owner/operator and
FFDG's separate DUNE role (pp3–4). It does not enumerate BMS points. The historical
FSCF-EXC transition plan, DocDB 24103 v1, pp11–12 identifies Oro Hondo and Ross
shaft responsibilities; section 5.1/p18 connects DUNE sump/drainage to mine drainage
and surface pumping. The diagram uses these for the vertical site-service context.

The actual FFDG building-service examples come from U-FD-M-901 rev12 and the
panel survey register: CUC/cavern HVAC and chilled-water interfaces at 4850;
SP-1/SP-2 at 4910. BSI section 230923 specifies Metasys and BACnet controller
networks. That field protocol is not a replacement for the selected API-to-SC
integration route. The map is a schematic; neither its lines nor the MOU prove
installed BMS controllers, point lists or software authority.

boxes this gives 400 readings. The former 1,280-reading case is superseded in

presentation names Opta and Beckhoff alternatives. The October PDS interface
presentation also describes a smaller 4–7-voltage + 1-RTD team proposal, but it
is Manuel's current four-reading instruction that fixes this deck's count.
PDU inhibition is the selected presentation path. A separate enable/inhibit
is optional pending the physical box interface. No controller grouping or new
terminal count is inferred from the monitoring population.
