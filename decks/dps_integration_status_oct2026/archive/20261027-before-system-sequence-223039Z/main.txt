DPS arbitrates protection; SC operates, monitors and logs

DPS                     Detector Protection System. Uses facility and detector conditions to allow or
                        block actions; issues permits, inhibits and trips.

SC                      Slow controls, using Ignition. Operates and monitors the detector; owns the
                        data and event logs, including DPS decisions.

BMS                     Building Management System. Controls building services. FFDG BMS and
                        SDSTA BMS are separate systems.

Local                   Equipment-level protection. Can trip without waiting for SC or the central DPS.
interlocks

An operating request cannot override a protection trip.

DPS receives inputs from four system domains

FFDG BMS                  API through the Batavia server at FNAL → SC → DPS.

SDSTA BMS                 Interface under investigation: available signals and connection points.

Cryogenics                Selected process conditions. Each inhibit names the actual instrument or con-
                          troller that supplies them.

Detector                  SC, the detector subsystems, and rack infrastructure: racks, PDUs and rack
                          sensors.

Example: low liquid level withdraws the HV permit. SC logs the condition, DPS decision and
equipment state.

The FFDG route is the working integration basis. SDSTA retains personnel-safety responsibility. Facility and cryogenic
systems retain control of their services.
