VD DPS source review — inhibit paths and rack infrastructure
27 October 2026

Result
The presentation connects the existing hardware to the procurement work:
trace each VD inhibit, identify its existing equipment and missing connections,
then count actual additions. It includes a separate rack-infrastructure system.
The review does not yet provide a final new-I/O count or bill of materials.

System roles and routes
Original DPS/SC/BMS/local-interlock definitions retained. FFDG uses the API
through Batavia and SC; SDSTA is under investigation. The single detector
overview includes OPC UA through SC, shared SNMP PDU/supply control, and
BDE/bias EtherCAT plus wired inhibits. The CUC location remains proposed.
SC owns the logs; bridge-server implementation stays outside the presentation.
Native protocol support does not establish an installed PLC implementation.

Existing VD hardware
The May VD workbook and June SC layout establish ten groups of eight WIECs,
20 PL506 supplies, 80 PTCs/WIECs and 480 WIBs. The displayed EDMS 2401090
panel is a historical reference with parts/power workbooks, not a verified
populated VD panel. No six-to-eight WIEC conversion is counted as new scope.
The HVS ICD also supplies a substantial inventory and cRIO/Heinzinger and
ISEG interface description. The source differences in crate housing/location
remain in the detailed FAC-01 review; they do not make HVS an undefined system.

BDE cooling example
The recovered WIEC note describes six individual fan power/return/RD circuits
on the DB25 connector. The ICEBERG hardware note documents EL1004 inputs with
pull-ups, EL2034 fan power/diagnostics, EL2124 5 V PL506 inhibit outputs and an
EL9505 output-bank supply. The VD architecture connects remote I/O over EtherCAT.
The path review identifies the fan inputs, PLC decision, wired inhibit and
supply output; exact VD terminal and WIEC/channel allocations remain unmapped.

The WIEC note is internal rev 0, modified 19 September 2025, from DocDB 34374
v1. It is not established as the released EDMS v2 attachment. Its six fans
differ from the four-fan ICEBERG reference. Assuming the six-fan interface
throughout gives 48 fan-status signals per eight-WIEC group, or 480 for VD.
These are baseline signal requirements, not additions or a terminal purchase.
The cooling rule, allowed time and failure/recovery behavior still need agreement.

Rack infrastructure
The rack, feeds, PDU(s), smoke/humidity sensors and support parts form one
system. Subsystem supplies and dedicated inhibits remain in their respective
inventories. DocDB 32655 v8 describes PX3, DX-PD2C5, 4WTAR-B and EOLR-1 plus
temperature/leak sensors and wiring. Humidity is included by Manuel; the guide
recognizes humidity readings but supplies no humidity-probe model or trip rule.

The guide is an Ignition-direct prototype with local fallback on loss of
Ignition. The proposed VD chain is Ignition -> DPS PLC -> PDU; it needs matching
trip/reachability/recovery configuration and tests. These are distinct maturity
states, not competing descriptions of the selected control ownership.

Hardware additions and alternatives
PDS LV remains 34. PoF remains 80 boxes with 1,280 working monitoring values.
June SC Progress slide 29 names Opta PLC and Beckhoff PLC alternatives. The
October architecture labels Berghof; no controller choice or box/controller
multiplicity is established by that label. Local signal types and actions
determine the I/O and parts for each option. TDE remains PDU-only now, with
future crate-status/external-inhibit options retained. CalCI uses its existing
instrumentation and calibration descriptions as the review starting point.

FFDG API points must be mapped to the conditions required by each rule.
The API connection itself consumes no discrete/analog DPS channels. SDSTA
hardware depends on the interface investigation. Cryogenic instrumentation
and process-control sources are kept distinct. FAC-01 illustrates the need
to map a specific alarm and its loads before assigning an extra facility input.

Panel space and power
The May sheet allocates 12U DPS per auxiliary/bias rack. It marks 19U Space
in auxiliary racks and 10U in bias racks; these are not new reservations.
Actual DIN-rail and electrical capacity follow the populated VD arrangement.
The first-pass bias layout gives 360 EL2124 / 1,440 outputs, not a confirmed
procurement quantity or verified spare capacity relative to 576 bias channels.

Expected PL506 input is 20 x 2,254 W = 45.08 kW under workbook assumptions.
At the six-fan note's 24 V, 0.33 A rating, fans alone are 8 x 6 x 0.33 x 24
= 380.16 W per auxiliary group. This is not the total panel load. The earlier
831 x 8/6 extrapolation is removed from the current power slide; the VD total
needs actual fans, heaters and I/O. Bias supply allowance is 960 W DC capacity.

Verification limits

and arithmetic checks do not establish wiring, settings, installed capacity
