DPS arbitrates protection; SC operates, monitors and logs

DPS                     Detector Protection System. Uses facility and detector conditions to allow or
                        block actions; issues permits, inhibits and trips.

SC                      Slow controls, using Ignition. Operates and monitors the detector; owns the
                        data and event logs, including DPS decisions.

BMS                     Building Management System. Controls building services. FFDG BMS and
                        SDSTA BMS are separate systems.

Local                   Equipment-level protection. Can trip without waiting for SC or the central DPS.
interlocks

An operating request cannot override a protection trip.
