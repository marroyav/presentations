# DPS hardware for vertical drift

Audience: the I&I workshop, including people outside controls.
Outcome: show the existing VD BDE hardware and agree the additions, engineering
support, tests and procurement work needed for the June 2027 review.

Scope: VD. HD may later use an extension or a separate PLC; its quantities are
excluded from the VD allocation.

Thirteen main slides including the title, plus one rack-inventory reference.
The deck now uses the May VD rack workbook, June SC/BDE layouts and screens,
the TPC panel/parts package, the PX3 setup guide and the subsystem inventories.
CUC placement, additions and work assignments are proposals. Counts describe
design allocations; source-specific limits are in the notes.

Read `source-review.txt` for the findings and their implications, and
`sources-and-notes.txt` for the slide-by-slide speaking notes. `sources.json`
records the source files, hashes and exact locations used in the review.

Build: `make decks/dps_integration_status_oct2026/main.pdf`.
Latest checks: the record named in `verification-latest.txt`.
Previous drafts are preserved in `archive/`; their old build records apply
only to those revisions.
