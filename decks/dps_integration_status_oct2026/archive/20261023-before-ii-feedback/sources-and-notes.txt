Connecting detector protection — presentation notes
23 October 2026

Purpose
Invite the collaboration to define protection requirements and interfaces.
The deck is a status and planning discussion, not an approved control design.

What to say
Slides 2–3: DPS protects equipment. SC operates and monitors it. BMS operates
building services. Cryogenic and facility conditions can affect whether the
detector may operate; each required signal needs an owner and a defined action.

Slide 4: The diagram is a proposed control rule, not installed wiring. Normal
SC commands and protection use separate paths. Local trips do not wait for
remote monitoring. Facility safety keeps its own authority. Response times
and the effect of a missing signal must be agreed for each function.

Slide 5: The team has collected documents, prepared inspection maps and drafted
control/interlock tables. A BMS access prototype exists; current-value use and
its service arrangement remain unfinished. Hardware models in drawings are
not a verified installed inventory. This status was recorded on 21 October.

Slide 6: Use the existing cryogenics work rather than inventing a second process
control system. The shared review charge gives a public starting point for
questions about signals, safety interfaces and failures. Restricted review
reports are deliberately excluded from the deck's citations.

Slide 7: The first two rows paraphrase the released interface. The lost-signal
row remains a decision. Each subsystem must define its operating stages,
limits, response time, failure behavior and reset/re-enable authority.

Slides 8–10: Ask each consortium for a contact and its interlock matrix.
Discuss shared conditions with SDSTA, LBNF and cryogenics. Agree the SC–DPS
scope and whether Fermilab takes full DPS accountability. Propose DPS review
of the SC subsystem interface documents. Start with one reviewed protection
path, then extend it. Publish BMS values as a separate monitoring service.

Terminology
SDSTA operates SURF. LBNF is the Long-Baseline Neutrino Facility project.
The deck does not claim that both building-management systems are new or
unbuilt. It asks to define their exchanges with DUNE before interfaces are fixed.
