# Connecting detector protection

Audience: DUNE collaboration members, including people outside controls.
Outcome: understand why detector protection needs facility information and
agree to provide contacts, protection rules and interface requirements.

Ten short slides: definitions, purpose, control authority, status, cryogenics,
interlock examples, consortium discussions, development goals and next actions.
The control scheme and next steps are proposals. Hardware availability and
installed signal paths remain unverified. Progress is recorded as of 21 October;
the presentation is dated 23 October 2026.

Build: `make decks/dps_integration_status_oct2026/main.pdf`.
The deck uses the shared DUNE theme and two editable TikZ block diagrams.
Online references and the distinction between facts and proposals are recorded
in `sources-and-notes.txt` and `sources.json`. The presentation contains no
local-file citations. The restricted cryogenics review reports are not cited;
the public review charge and public SC–DPS interface supply the shared basis.
