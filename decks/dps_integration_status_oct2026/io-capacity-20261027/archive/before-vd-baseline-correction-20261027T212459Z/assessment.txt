Slide 6: space and module additions for VD
27 October 2026 — engineering estimate, not a released panel design

There is room in the rack allocations for extension panels. The 12U already
assigned to BDE is occupied by its protection equipment; it is not 12U of
spare mounting area. The old panel drawing is crowded. Reserve 4U in each
affected auxiliary rack as an initial extension allowance and lay out the
actual terminals, supplies, cable ducts and connectors within that allowance.

Space supported by the sources

The May 2025 FD2 rack workbook has ten auxiliary racks. BDE Aux Rack!C14
assigns 12U to DPS, covering U22–U33. Rows 26–44 explicitly label U3–U21
as Space: a contiguous 19U block per rack. A note at U9 suggests possible
ground-plane readout, so the entire block is not a dedicated DPS reservation.

Each of ten bias racks also assigns 12U to DPS (Bias HV Rack!C15). The
contiguous block U3–U12 is marked Space: 10U per rack. K17:K18 describe two
rails and 337 mm of equipment on each. The usable rail length is not given;
subtracting 337 mm from the full 19-inch panel width would ignore margins,
supports, cable access and terminals.

EDMS 2401090 v4, F10xxxxxx-Prelim_-_DDSS_Controls.pdf, sheet 2, shows:
- A 4U central PLC panel using CX5120. Scaling the visible gap against the
  19-inch dimension gives about 85–90 mm. The later parts list selects
  CX5140, about 20 mm wider, leaving roughly 65–70 mm if the other positions
  are unchanged: about five 12 mm terminals geometrically. This is an
  estimate from the drawing, not a dimensioned or installed spare allowance.
- A 12U TPC auxiliary panel. The upper I/O row is almost full; the lower row
  contains the fibre junctions and power supply. The drawing uses the older
  output-module arrangement and cannot be treated as the as-built layout of
  the later parts list.
- An 8U bias reference panel. Do not confuse that historical drawing with
  the later VD workbook's 12U, two-rail allocation.

The workbook itself says the PD flange addition brings DIN-rail use close
to the maximum (BDE Aux Rack!K20:K21). This is evidence against claiming
large spare terminal capacity from the slide 6 drawing.

VD rebase of the existing six-WIEC parts pattern

The April 2023 CE Interlock Parts List uses 25 six-WIEC groups. Divide its
production quantities by 25, then apply the same allocation to eight WIECs
per auxiliary rack. This is an explicit scaling assumption, not evidence
that the VD fan/heater grouping has already been approved.

This preserves four fan feedbacks, four fan outputs, two RTDs and four
heater outputs per WIEC from that parts pattern. The BDE ICD section 5
still leaves fan/heater inhibit grouping to the consortium. Two existing
EL2124 terminals provide eight inhibit outputs for the eight active PL506
feeds in a VD group. Covering all twelve supply-channel positions would
instead add one more EL2124 per group; unused positions are not counted here.

The nine added modules occupy about 130 mm of aligned rail length in total:
seven 12 mm terminals and two 23 mm EK1521 fibre junctions. That total is
spread over the signal and fibre banks; it is not one continuous free gap.

The workbook's PD-flange option adds 3 EL3202, 2 EL2878 and 1 EL9100 per
auxiliary rack: six more modules, about 72 mm, or 60 modules across ten racks.
This option is quoted as written. The workbook flags its inherited six-WIEC
calculation; the actual VD PD flange population could change the option.

Power sets another limit

Using the 2023 module mix scaled to eight WIECs, the first bank's signal
terminals draw about 3.86 A of typical E-bus current. Its EK1100 and one
EL9410 provide two separately limited 2 A sections. The PD option adds
0.77 A, taking the signal-terminal total to 4.63 A. The combined option
therefore needs at least one additional E-bus supply section, such as
another EL9410, even before any new facility I/O. The order of terminals
must keep each section within its individual limit. These are typical
electronics currents, not the 24 V fan/heater load calculation.

The fibre bank is separate: eight EK1521 at 350 mA each total 2.8 A, which
can be split four and four across its existing two 2 A sections. This does
not establish unused current in the signal bank.

Planning counts per affected auxiliary rack are therefore:
- VD rebase only: nine added signal/link modules, before any additional
  power-feed allowance needed by the final arrangement.
- VD rebase plus the workbook PD option: fifteen added modules.
- The same combined case plus one extra E-bus feed: sixteen, or 160 across
  ten racks; 214 mm of added aligned module width distributed over the banks.
No spare-parts allowance, new coupler/end cap for a separate station, new
power supply, terminal block, fuse, connector or cable duct is included.

Other DPS additions

The stated architecture uses SNMP for PDUs and LV supplies and OPC UA for
additional subsystem exchange through SC. Those connections require no
additional discrete or analog I/O terminals merely because another device
or software value is added. The 34 PDS LV feeds, 20 TDE racks and 1,280 PoF
readings are not corresponding counts of central PLC terminal channels.

The remaining physical additions are the selected facility and equipment
contacts or signals: for example a facility alarm, cryogenic permit or HV
inhibit. The current integration matrix names functions but does not give
a complete wired channel allocation. A final module total for those
interfaces is not supported yet. The counts above quantify the available
BDE pattern and a documented option without inventing that channel map.
