Slide 6: space and I/O additions beyond the VD BDE baseline
27 October 2026 — corrected after Manuel's scope clarification

The starting point is VD: ten BDE auxiliary groups, eight WIECs per group,
80 WIECs and 20 PL506 supplies. The existing BDE protection hardware belongs
to that baseline. No HD-to-VD conversion is part of the new DPS scope.

Correction to the previous estimate

The earlier nine-module difference per group answered an HD-to-VD adaptation
question. Its 90-, 150- and 160-module totals do not answer how much hardware
is needed beyond the VD BDE system. Those totals are withdrawn. The related
4U extension recommendation and definite extra E-bus feed claim are also
withdrawn: they relied on assigning the historical panel arrangement to VD.
The earlier calculation is preserved under archive/ as superseded evidence.

What the VD workbook establishes about rack space

BDE Aux Rack!C14 allocates 12U to DPS in each of ten auxiliary racks. The
cells A26:B44 mark U3–U21 as Space: a contiguous 19U block per rack. U9 also
has a possible ground-plane readout note. These positions are unassigned
in this workbook, not newly reserved for DPS.

Bias HV Rack!C15 allocates 12U to DPS in each of ten bias racks. A36:B45
marks U3–U12 as Space: a contiguous 10U block per rack. K17:K18 describe two
rails with 337 mm occupied on each. The usable rail length is not specified.

Rack space and free DIN-rail length are distinct. The historical panel
shown on slide 6 does not establish spare terminal capacity after the VD
baseline equipment is placed. Its visible gaps must not be quoted as
verified spare space in the VD panels.

What extending the VD DPS adds

- Existing VD BDE protection: zero new modules from changing the group size;
  eight WIECs per group is already the baseline.
- PDU and LV-supply SNMP exchange: zero added discrete or analog DPS I/O
  terminals for the network connection itself.
- Additional subsystem OPC UA exchange through SC: zero added discrete or
  analog DPS terminals for that network connection itself.
- Additional wired facility or equipment inputs, permits and inhibits:
  count these from the VD signal allocation. The reviewed source matrix
  names functions but does not establish a complete wired channel count.
- Support hardware: derive any additional bus feeds, couplers, supplies and
  terminal blocks from the actual VD bank arrangement and these new channels.

The workbook records an optional PD-flange package in BDE Aux Rack!K20:K21:
3 EL3202 + 2 EL2878 + 1 EL9100, six modules. The same notes identify inherited
HD assumptions. Retain this as a source option; its applicability and number
of instances in VD are not established. Do not multiply it by ten or count
it as a new VD requirement without the VD flange/interface allocation.

The 34 PDS LV feeds, 20 TDE racks and 1,280 PoF monitoring values remain
system populations. They are not counts of new central DPS terminal channels.
This says nothing about the hardware needed for local subsystem readout.

Result

The VD workbook supports rack-space figures of 19U per auxiliary rack and
10U per bias rack marked Space. It does not establish a new reservation.
The corrected additional physical I/O total and spare VD panel capacity
remain unset. Zero applies to the network connections themselves, not to
all future hardware additions. A per-rack extension size cannot yet be
justified by the withdrawn HD conversion calculation.
