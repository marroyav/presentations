PDS PoF budget — one slide — 14 October 2026

Both comparisons have 40 racks, two boxes per rack and one normal enable
per box. Prices are published single-unit USD retail/reseller listings captured
14 October 2026, not Beckhoff factory prices, a project quote or a landed cost.
Ranges reflect the source listings, not a quantified uncertainty interval.

SCOPE 1: up to 7 V + one two-wire PT100 RTD per box.
The PoF team presentation reports 4–7 voltages and one chassis RTD; its
figure shows two boxes connected through Burndy connectors and a terminal block
to one Opta and an analog expansion. Cost the maximum of seven voltages.
Opta: one AFX00003 Lite plus one AFX00007 analog expansion per rack. The
base contributes eight voltage inputs; the expansion supplies the remaining
six voltage and two RTD inputs. Two of four base relays provide box enables.
Cost $379.50–395.35/rack; $15,180–15,814 across 40 racks.
Beckhoff: one EL3208 for two RTDs, two EL3068 for 14 voltages and one shared
EL1859 for the box enables. Cost $1,269.38–1,499.38/rack;
$50,775.20–59,975.20 across 40 racks.

SCOPE 2: user working inventory, 8 V + 8 T per box.
Opta: two AFX00003 Lite plus two AFX00007 analog expansions per rack. Each
base reads eight voltages; its expansion reads eight two-wire PT100 RTDs.
Each box uses one relay for its enable. This is capacity-based sizing, not
what the source presentation reports testing. Cost $759–790.70/rack;
$30,360–31,628 for 40 racks. One CPU plus three analog expansions could
also cover these inputs, at $836.90/rack using the lower listed prices;
two CPU/expansion pairs are cheaper at those unit prices.
Beckhoff: two EL3208 + two EL3068 + one shared EL1859 per rack.
Cost $1,624.38–2,084.38/rack; $64,975.20–83,375.20 across 40 racks.
The indicative hardware premium is $33,347.20–53,015.20 across 40 racks,
combining the lowest Beckhoff total with the highest Opta total and vice versa.

COST BOUNDARY
The existing Beckhoff CPU/OPC UA platform is provided at zero incremental CPU
cost. The flange-side EtherCAT route is already in project scope. Extra
couplers, end terminals, station power and spare capacity still need a final
BOM. These are excluded, together with taxes/tariffs, shipping, volume
discounts, sensors, connectors, cabling, conditioning, integration engineering,
software validation, long-term support and independent DPS/protection I/O.
Opta relay contacts and Beckhoff 24 V solid-state outputs require the matching
box-enable electrical interface. The source does not show an enable pinout.
Beckhoff’s integration and maintenance reuse is a separate engineering benefit;
no monetary saving for it has been assumed.

TECHNICAL SOURCES
Arduino AFX00007 datasheet, pp.5–6: eight programmable input channels;
all eight accept two-wire PT100 RTDs. Base Opta: eight voltage inputs and four
relay outputs. Official source URLs are in estimates.json.
